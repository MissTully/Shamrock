# Shop Studio

The merchandise chair signs in to **Member Hub → Officer desk → Shop Studio**. Create or edit a product, add its price, sizes, description, and image (paste a public image URL or upload one), then choose a status. A priced product saved without a Zeffy URL is automatically marked **awaiting Zeffy**; the Shop Studio list notes that automation will create the campaign. Paste the campaign URL later or use **Attach Zeffy link**.

Products marked **live** with a Zeffy URL appear in `store.html` with a Buy now link. Coming-soon and awaiting-Zeffy products remain visible with an explanatory status. The treasurer sees completed shop sales through the existing `zeffy-webhook` payment ledger integration.
