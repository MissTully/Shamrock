Service & Charity

Giving our time and treasure to Tampa Bay's children and families.

More Than a Parade

For more than twenty-five years, the Krewe of Shamrock has been a force for good in Tampa Bay. Our members are compassionate, caring people who give their time and their treasure, donating thousands of dollars and countless volunteer hours to charities across the community.

Every full member commits to 12 volunteer hours a year, and most give far more. Service is woven into who we are.
