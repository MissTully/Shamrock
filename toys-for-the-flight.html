Krewe of Shamrock · Christmas toy drive

Toys for the Flight

A Christmas toy drive for Johns Hopkins All Children's LifeLine Transport

Learn about LifeLine →
kreweofshamrock.com/toys-for-the-flight.html

Comfort on the way

LifeLine moves critically ill and injured children by ambulance, helicopter, and fixed-wing aircraft around the clock. Toys help distract and comfort kids during scary transports.

A small, quiet toy that fits a jump bag can turn a frightening ride into a moment of calm. That is what this drive is for.

LifeLine Transport Team

What to donate

Think small, new, wipeable, and quiet

Please bring

  • New only
  • Plastic and wipeable
  • Small / handheld (fits a jump bag)
  • Quiet and comforting — figurines, rattles, soft plastic teethers, small books, quiet puzzles, sensory toys
  • Ages infant through teen

Please avoid

  • Used toys
  • Plush when possible
  • Loud electronics
  • Food or candy
  • Choking hazards
