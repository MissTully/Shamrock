Welcome. Your membership is approved, subject to final ratification by the Board of Directors, and you are on the roster.

To use the Member Hub, create your login with the same email you put on the application:

  1. Open the Member Hub at kreweofshamrock.com/members.html.
  2. Tap Create or reset your password.
  3. Enter that email. We will send you a secure link. The same link works for a first password or a reset.
  4. Open the link on your device and choose a password of at least 8 characters.
  5. Come back and sign in with that email and password.

Signing up with this email links your login to your membership. Old Wild Apricot passwords do not work here.

When you are signed in, watch the member website training video: Member Hub training.
