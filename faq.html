How many beads will I need?

This is really an individual answer. You will need more for Gasparilla day as this is the longest parade. Maybe a case for this one — 60 dozen of the standard 33' beads, 30–40 dozen for the other parades. The number is dependent upon your throwing style — do you try to throw constantly? Also, if you get more expensive beads, you won't want to throw indiscriminately.

Charities

How many volunteer hours are required

12 volunteer hours are required per member or $12 per hour in lieu of actual volunteering. Volunteer hours can also consist of in-kind donations — for every $12 worth of donation, you can log one volunteer hour.

Ways to earn volunteer hours

You will have the opportunity to volunteer at a Krewe of Shamrock organized event or other sponsored events. Details will be on the Krewe of Shamrock website, www.kreweofshamrock.com (Service & Charity and Upcoming Events), or the Facebook page.

  • Float Work Day — Krewe members are encouraged to volunteer to clean the float after a parade. This is usually scheduled on the Sunday after the parade. Cleaning supplies are on the float and a group of people is required for this.
  • Parade Security (will receive double hours if an alcohol parade)
  • In kind donation of $12 per hour (liquor, raffle items, cash, etc.)

How to record volunteer hours

Please keep in mind, even if you have all 12 hours completed, it is good to still keep track of all additional hours going forward.

On this site:

  1. Log in.
  2. Open Member desk.
  3. Log volunteer hours.

There is a separate login required for the volunteer tracking page. Reach out to your sponsor if you have not received this email.

Attire for Parades

What to wear

Everyone must wear kilts unless you are on security detail. You can wear a Krewe shirt with the kilt if you want or see below. Keep in mind, we are a Celtic Krewe, not a pirate one. No pirate hats, etc. Also, do not wear anything with any offensive or political statements — you are representing the whole Krewe.

Women

  • Kilt is required for all parades.
  • Christmas/festive top for the Santa parade.
  • For Gasparilla or Knight parade, google renaissance, pirate or Celtic tops — there are tons of options on Amazon. You can also pair with a corset.
  • St. Patrick's Parade — wear green, be festive or wear your Celtic attire.
  • Many women wear tights or stockings.
  • Shoes — wear comfortable foot attire. Most wear some kind of walking boot.
  • Cold weather — layer! Get thermal tights, long underwear, etc. Just be comfortable.
  • A sporran or fanny pack is a good idea to carry your cell phone, keys, etc.

Men

  • Kilt is required for all parades.
  • Christmas/festive shirt for the Santa parade.
  • For Gasparilla or Knight parade, google ghillie, renaissance or Celtic shirt. You can also wear a Krewe T-shirt.
  • St. Patrick's Parade — wear green, be festive or wear your Celtic attire.
  • Feet — kilt hose (socks) and a pair of comfortable shoes — many wear some kind of walking boot.
  • Cold weather — layer! Get thermal tights, long underwear, etc. Just be comfortable.
  • A sporran or fanny pack is a good idea to carry your cell phone, keys, etc.
  • An Irish beret or tartan wrap are fun for head attire.

Guests, conduct, and security

Can I bring guests

Generally, guests can be invited, for a fee, to non-alcohol events only. The president is allowed guests at all parades.

You are 100% responsible for your guests. All guests are to wear visible wristbands and the sponsor is to be present.

Behavior

You are representing the Krewe of Shamrock! Indecent/vulgar behavior or drunkenness is not tolerated.

No smoking on the float — this includes vapes.

Nametags

The wearing of nametags is strongly encouraged.

Security

If you are working security for a parade, you will be provided with a vest. There will be a briefing before each parade with instructions. Anyone on security detail cannot drink alcohol while working. If they are seen drinking, the whole Krewe may be prohibited from participating in parades in the future.
