Every application needs a background check, a current member as sponsor, and Board approval. No sponsor yet? Apply anyway; we'll invite you along to meet the krewe.

Application fee (background check)

Single applicant: $50. Couple (two applicants): $75. This is the application fee. It is not membership dues. The payment link is not published on this page. After you submit, the Membership Chair sends the next step.

What happens next

  1. Submit this form. Your application starts as new.
  2. Pay the application fee when the Membership Chair sends that step. $50 for one person, or $75 for a couple. That fee is the background check.
  3. The chair moves the application to background check in progress.
  4. After the background check, membership dues come due. Full krewe is $375. Leave of absence is $100. Those links below are membership dues, not the application fee.
