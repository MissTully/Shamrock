Krewe of Unicorns Raffle

Pick a basket, grab your tickets, and you're in the draw ✨

🧺 How the Basket Raffle works

  1. Browse the prize baskets below.
  2. Enter your name and how many tickets you want for that basket.
  3. Each ticket is one entry in that basket, so load up on the ones you want most!
  4. At the end, one ticket per basket is drawn at random. More tickets = better odds.

💰 How the 50/50 works

  1. Buy 50/50 tickets in any amount.
  2. All tickets go into one pot.
  3. One ticket is drawn at the end.
  4. The winner takes half the pot; the other half supports the Krewe's charities.
Paying: Tickets are paid in person. Settle up with any Krewe volunteer at the table (cash or card). Your entries are recorded the moment you submit, and a volunteer will confirm payment. Sparkle on! 🌈
