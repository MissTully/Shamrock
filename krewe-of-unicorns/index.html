A Toast to Wonder

The spiral horn, the alicorn, has shimmered through the myths of nearly every culture for thousands of years: a symbol of purity, healing, and the belief that something magical is always just over the next hill. Like the rainbow it chases, our krewe has no beginning and no end: just neighbors, music, and the joy of a little wonder shared hand to hand.

Whether you were born believing or simply love good music and a good party, there's a place for you in the herd.
