Why Sponsor the Ball?

Presented by the Krewe of Shamrock, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit

Every sponsorship puts your name in front of Tampa Bay’s krewe community on our most elegant night of the year. Sponsors are featured on event signage, thanked from the stage during the 7:00 PM dinner presentations, and celebrated in our social media. Because the Krewe of Shamrock is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, your payment receipt doubles as your tax receipt.
