## Photo & Image Release (Member Media Consent)

I, the undersigned member of the **Krewe of Shamrock** (“Krewe”), grant the Krewe permission to create, use, reproduce, display, and distribute photographs, video, audio, and other images or recordings that include me (and, if applicable, my minor child named below) for Krewe purposes.

### 1. What this covers
This consent covers photos, videos, livestreams, and similar media taken or collected in connection with Krewe activities, including but not limited to:

- Gasparilla and other parades  
- St. Patrick’s Day and Irish heritage events  
- Tartan Ball and formal krewe events  
- Meetings, volunteer service, fundraisers, and social gatherings  
- Member-uploaded content to the Krewe website / member hub  

### 2. How the Krewe may use the media
The Krewe may use such media for nonprofit krewe purposes, including:

- kreweofshamrock.com (including the Photo Gallery, events pages, and member features)  
- Related Krewe digital channels (email newsletters, member communications, official social accounts)  
- Print materials such as programs, flyers, posters, and banners  
- Inter-Krewe Council (IKC) and community listings that promote the Krewe of Shamrock  
- Historical archives of the Krewe  

The Krewe will **not** sell member likeness rights to unrelated third parties for commercial advertising without a separate written agreement.

### 3. Ownership of Krewe-created media
Media created by or for the Krewe (official photographers, officers, or contractors working for the Krewe) may be retained by the Krewe as part of its records and promotional materials. Member-uploaded photos and videos remain subject to this consent when posted to Krewe channels.

### 4. No payment
I understand that I will not receive payment for this consent or for the Krewe’s use of my likeness under this release.

### 5. Removal requests
I understand I may ask an officer (Digital or Secretary) to remove a **specific** post or image from the public website or official social channels when reasonably practical. Removal from printed materials already distributed, third-party shares, or archival backups may not always be possible.

### 6. Duration
This consent remains in effect for my membership and for reasonable archival and historical use afterward, unless I revoke it in writing to `digital@kreweofshamrock.com` or `secretary@kreweofshamrock.com`. Revocation applies going forward and does not require removal of materials already lawfully published before the Krewe receives the revocation, except as the Krewe agrees in good faith for a specific item.

### 7. Minors (if applicable)
If I am signing for a minor: I am the parent or legal guardian of the minor named below, and I grant the same permissions on their behalf.

### 8. Acknowledgement
I have read this Photo & Image Release, understand it, and sign it voluntarily.
