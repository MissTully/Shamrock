Photo & Image Release

Krewe of Shamrock · Tampa Bay · digital@kreweofshamrock.com · secretary@kreweofshamrock.com

Working member media consent for nonprofit krewe use. Review with an officer or counsel before relying on it for commercial licensing outside krewe purposes.

I, the undersigned member of the Krewe of Shamrock (“Krewe”), grant the Krewe permission to create, use, reproduce, display, and distribute photographs, video, audio, and other images or recordings that include me (and, if applicable, my minor child named below) for Krewe purposes.

1. What this covers

Photos, videos, livestreams, and similar media from Krewe activities, including Gasparilla and other parades; St. Patrick’s Day and Irish heritage events; Tartan Ball and formal events; meetings, volunteer service, fundraisers, and social gatherings; and member-uploaded content to the Krewe website / member hub.

2. How the Krewe may use the media

  • kreweofshamrock.com (Photo Gallery, events pages, member features)
  • Official Krewe email, newsletters, and social channels
  • Print programs, flyers, posters, and banners
  • Inter-Krewe Council (IKC) and community listings that promote the Krewe
  • Historical archives of the Krewe

The Krewe will not sell member likeness rights to unrelated third parties for commercial advertising without a separate written agreement.

3. Ownership of Krewe-created media

Media created by or for the Krewe may be retained as krewe records and promotional materials. Member-uploaded photos and videos are covered by this consent when posted to Krewe channels.

4. No payment

I will not receive payment for this consent or for the Krewe’s use of my likeness under this release.

5. Removal requests

I may ask Digital or the Secretary to remove a specific post or image from the public website or official social channels when reasonably practical. Printed materials already distributed, third-party shares, or archival backups may not always be removable.

6. Duration

This consent remains in effect for my membership and for reasonable archival use afterward, unless I revoke it in writing to digital@kreweofshamrock.com or secretary@kreweofshamrock.com. Revocation applies going forward.

7. Minors

If signing for a minor, I am the parent or legal guardian and grant the same permissions on their behalf.

8. Acknowledgement

I have read this Photo & Image Release, understand it, and sign it voluntarily.
