A Toast to Tradition

The triquetra, the Celtic knot of three, has wound its way through Irish art for over a thousand years. Like its endless line, our krewe has no beginning and no end: just neighbors, music, and the warmth of a shared heritage passed hand to hand.

Whether your roots run to Cork or Kilkenny or you simply love good music and a good party, there's a place for you at our table.
