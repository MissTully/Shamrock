Dues & Leave of Absence

How members stay in good standing for the season (Jun–May volunteer year)

Member dues

Annual dues keep you in good standing for meetings, parade eligibility, and Member Hub access. Pay online through Zeffy, then check Parade Ready on Member Desk for your paid status.

Pay member dues

Leave of absence (LOA)

If you need a season away from full participation, the Krewe offers an LOA option at $100. Use this when you are not marching or meeting the usual volunteer path but want to stay connected as a member.

Pay LOA ($100)

Volunteer hours

Full members contribute 12 volunteer hours for the Krewe year (June through May), or $12 per hour, including in-kind donations. Log hours in Member Desk. A host or an officer confirms them. If hours from earlier this season still need to come over, enter them once as season hours on file, or ask an officer to import the past export.
