How to use it: print these twelve pages and you have a paper season planner. Krewe events and our parades are already in their squares; the dotted Pencil it in lines and the roomy day squares are for everything announced later — write events in as they're added. Other krewes' (IKC) events aren't pre-printed on purpose, to keep your writing room; find them on the Hub events calendar.
