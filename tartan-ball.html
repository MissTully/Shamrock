The Night We Gathered

Presented by the Krewe of Shamrock

Celtic Elegance

Higgins Hall held an evening of tradition, celebration, and distinction as the Krewe of Shamrock honored the ceremonial crowning of our new King & Queen. Candlelight and chandeliers, the skirl of the pipes, and royal Irish splendor with the finest company in Tampa Bay.

Black tie and tartan filled the room, from the first photograph to the last reel.

The Evening’s Program

How the night unfolded

6:00 to 7:00 PM. Cocktail hour with pictures and the Moon Coin band

7:00 to 8:00 PM. Dinner with presentations

8:00 to 10:00 PM. Dancing, the 50/50, and the raffle

10:00 PM. The bar closed, and the 50/50, raffles, and liquor cart were drawn and awarded

An Evening of Dancing & Finery

The Tartan Ball is our most elegant night of the year: a grand ballroom, a turn around the floor, and the warmth of old friends. Formal attire with a Celtic touch carried the room: a sash, a tartan, a kilt, or the sharpest black-tie green.

From the first waltz to the last reel, it was a night to dress up, dance, and celebrate together.

The Setting

Candlelight and chandeliers in a grand Tampa ballroom: fine dining, music, and the ceremonial crowning of our new King & Queen.

With Thanks to Our Sponsors

The Moon Coin band, the photography, the Irish dancers, and the liquor wagon were part of the night because sponsors stood behind them. Their names were on the signage, they were thanked during the 7:00 PM dinner presentations, and they received a social media shoutout.
