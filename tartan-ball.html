An Evening of Dancing & Finery

The Tartan Ball is our most elegant night of the year — a grand ballroom, a turn around the floor, and the warmth of old friends. Think formal attire with a Celtic touch: a sash, a tartan, a kilt, or your sharpest black-tie green.

From the first waltz to the last reel, it’s a night to dress up, dance, and celebrate together.
