# Céilí Website Demo: 5-Minute Narration Script

**Runtime:** just under 5:00 at a relaxed voiceover pace (about 145 words per minute, ~680
spoken words plus pauses), leaving a little headroom for a slower read. The `<break>` tags are
ElevenLabs pause markup; paste the block below straight into ElevenLabs as-is.

**What was cut from the long version:** the entire rules and documentation appendix (roles and
permissions, Craic Cup point tables, raffle and dues rules, privacy, officer administration,
roadmap). That material is reference documentation, not narration; it lives in `PRODUCT.md`
and `DATABASE_BACKEND.md`. The walkthrough was also updated for the current site: Krewe
Creativity (formerly Poetry & Art), the Heritage Library, and the Parade Ready card, none of
which were in the original script.

---

```
Hi everyone! I'm Melissa Tully, and welcome to the new home of the Krewe of Shamrock,
Tampa Bay's original Irish krewe. All for Fun, Fun for All. Let me give you the
five-minute tour. <break time="0.7s" />

This is our homepage. Right up top, the menu takes you everywhere: Events, Parades,
Learn, Krewe Creativity, Gallery, Volunteer, the Shop, Join, and the Members Area.
<break time="0.5s" />

Let's start with Events. The whole season lives here, from the summer picnic to the
Tartan Ball to every parade in between. Click any event, add your guest count, and
you're RSVP'd in seconds. When an officer publishes an event, it shows up on the
homepage, the calendar, and the signup page all at once. Publish once, and the whole
krewe knows. <break time="0.5s" />

Over on Parades, meet the Castle of Shenanigans, the float our own members designed
and built with their own hands in 2019, sound system, bar, rails, and that Leprechaun
green skull. You'll find the story of the build, plus all six parades we roll in
every season, from SantaFest in December to the Rough Riders' Saint Patrick's parade
in March. And when the season calls for something fancier, the Tartan Ball page has
your formal night covered. <break time="0.5s" />

The Learn page is our Heritage Library. Seven interactive lessons with videos, music,
and quizzes, starting with kilts, because at this krewe, kilts come first, Irish or
Scottish tradition, your choice. Below that,
fourteen story cards on everything from Saint Patrick to the legends of old Ireland,
with a progress bar that tracks what you've explored. <break time="0.5s" />

Krewe Creativity is our community stage. Members share photography, music, video,
art, and writing here, and you can play a tune like the Céilí Hearth right on the
page. <break time="0.5s" />

Gallery and Videos hold the proof of all that fun, and the Volunteer page is where
you sign up for service events like the food warehouse and the toy drive. The Shop
has your krewe gear. <break time="0.5s" />

Thinking of joining? The Join page lays it all out: membership tiers, dues, the
twelve-hour volunteer tradition, and how sponsorship and Board approval work.
<break time="0.7s" />

Now for the good stuff. The Members Area. <break time="0.5s" /> There's no password
to remember. Type your email, click the magic link we send you, and you're in. Only
emails on the krewe roster work, so it stays members-only. <break time="0.5s" />

The first thing you see is your Parade Ready card. It tracks the three things you
need to march: dues paid, waiver signed, and your mandatory meeting. Sign the waiver
right on the card in about ten seconds. Three green checks, and you're parade ready.
You can also log your volunteer hours here and watch the bar fill toward twelve.
<break time="0.5s" />

At meetings, we put a QR code on the screen. Scan it with your phone, and your
attendance records itself. No clipboard, no sign-in sheet. <break time="0.5s" />

Then there's the Craic Cup, our season-long friendly rivalry. You earn Clovers for
RSVPing, showing up, volunteering, and bringing guests, and the points add up all
season. Climb the ranks from Newcomer all the way to Highland Champion, collect
badges along the way, and check the leaderboard to see who's winning bragging rights
this year. <break time="0.5s" />

Raffles live here too. Buy your basket tickets and the fifty-fifty right from your
phone at any event. <break time="0.5s" />

Members can also rent a locker, join a carpool, grab a van-pool seat, and look up
fellow members in the directory. And if you're an officer, the Reports dashboard has
every answer two clicks away: the wristband pickup list, volunteer hour approvals,
dues summaries, headcounts, and more. <break time="0.5s" />

Got something to share? Submit your photos, videos, art, or stories right from the
Members Area, and an officer reviews it before it goes live on the site. When you're
done, sign out is one click away. <break time="0.7s" />

And that's the tour. One home for our stories, our events, and our people, built by
this krewe, for this krewe. Come for the parades, stay for the family. All for Fun,
Fun for All. <break time="0.5s" /> Sláinte!
```
