Our new website,
and what it does for you

One place for the whole krewe: events, parades, the Tartan Ball, our heritage, and the everyday business of belonging — dues, waivers, rides, lockers, and volunteering — all from the phone in your pocket.

The public side of the site is live tonight — scan the code and have a look. The Members Area is built and locked down; it opens to you by invitation over the next few weeks, in small batches, so nothing breaks on the way in.

Until then the site is in transition. Pages will shift, content will keep landing, and a few things from the old site are still being carried across. We will tell you plainly when it has landed.

What you get

Every member

Everything in one place

Events, parades, the Tartan Ball, our heritage and history, the photo gallery, videos, Krewe Creativity, and the shop — no more hunting through email threads.

RSVP in a few taps

Say you're coming, add your guests, done. Your officers see the count immediately.

Parade Ready — know where you stand

One screen shows your dues, your season waiver, and your mandatory meeting, and tells you whether you're clear for a wristband. Sign the waiver right there.

Log your own volunteer hours

Put in the hours you worked and they're on your record — no need to catch an officer after a parade.

The Craic Cup

Earn Clovers for showing up and pitching in, climb the ranks, collect badges, and watch the season standings and top-volunteer board. Recognition and bragging rights, nothing more serious than that.

Rides, van pools & lockers

Offer a ride or ask for one, reserve a van pool seat, and rent a locker — all without a single group text.

Raffles

50/50 and basket raffles run from the Members Area, with the baskets and winners posted where everyone can see them.

Member directory

Names and roles of active members. Your phone number, email, and personal details stay private — the directory does not show them.

Share what you've made

Send in your photos, videos, poems, and artwork. Once published they appear on the site for the whole krewe.

No password to remember

You sign in with a one-time link emailed to you. Nothing to invent, nothing to forget, nothing to have stolen.

What it saves your officers

Board & committee chairs

One roster, one source of truth

Members, dues, events, and signups live together instead of across three spreadsheets and someone's inbox.

Dues reminders that send themselves

A weekly check finds who is unpaid, sends a polite first notice and firmer ones after, and logs every nudge so nobody gets asked twice in a week.

A reports dashboard

Who is ready for wristbands, and who is missing what — at a glance, on parade morning, instead of a clipboard.

Meetings with QR check‑in

Schedule a meeting, mark it mandatory, and check members in by scanning. Attendance flows straight into Parade Ready.

Applications that process themselves

A prospect applies online; approving them creates their first-year dues record and sends the welcome email automatically.

Raffle tools, officer‑only

Create and manage baskets, run the 50/50, and draw winners — with the controls locked to officers.

New content without a developer

Add a photo, a video, or a heritage lesson and it shows on the live site in seconds. No code, no waiting on anyone.

Privacy built into the plumbing

Only officers can see the full roster and dues. A member sees their own record and the directory — that rule is enforced by the database itself, not by hiding a button.

The weeks ahead

What to expect, in order
Now — tonight

Browse the public site

Everything outside the Members Area is open. Look around, and expect it to keep changing week to week as content lands and a few remaining pages from the old site — Charities, Leaders & Board, and Contact — are carried across.

Next — behind the scenes

Getting the plumbing ready

Turning on email delivery, moving the database onto its own paid plan, clearing out the test data, and setting up a proper web address of our own. None of this is visible to you, and all of it has to be right before we let 80 people in.

Then

Invitations, in small batches

Officers and a handful of members first, then the rest of the krewe in waves — deliberately staggered so the email doesn't get throttled and so we catch any problem on a few people instead of everyone. Your invitation comes to the email address the krewe has on file for you.

Your turn

Your first sign‑in

Request your one-time link, set up your profile, and take the short welcome tour. Five minutes, once.

Landing

We'll say when it's done

You'll get a clear announcement when the site is fully live and the Members Area is open to everyone. Until you hear that, treat what you see as work in progress.

Two things we need from you tonight

Confirm your email address

Check the roster sheet before you leave. If we have the wrong address, or an old one, your invitation goes nowhere — and that is the single most common reason a member gets left behind.

Tell us what's missing

Scan the code, poke at the site, and tell an officer what's wrong, what's confusing, or what you'd like to see. It is far easier to fix now than after launch.
